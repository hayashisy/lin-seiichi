Problem I Solve

Challenges I Help You Overcome

Procurement in China comes with three barriers: quality, delivery, and communication. I clear all three — with on-the-ground experience and a perspective that bridges both countries.

  • 🔍 Unstable qualityI audit factories in person — checking drawings against actual parts, evaluating against Japanese standards.
  • 📅 Missed delivery deadlinesI track production progress on-site, spotting delay risks early and taking corrective action.
  • 💬 Miscommunication and language barriersI convey technical requirements precisely in Japanese — nuance included.
  • 💰 Uncertain pricing fairnessWith deep market knowledge, I scrutinize quotes to protect you from overcharging.

    01  Supplier Sourcing & Auditing
